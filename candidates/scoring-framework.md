# Candidate Scoring Framework

Per Guardrail 7, every candidate channel idea is scored on the dimensions below.
Weights reflect the brief's priorities: monetization ($2,000/mo AdSense in 6 months — G11),
rankability for a brand-new channel (G10), and solo sustainability at 1–2 videos/week (G12).

## Dimensions (100 pts core + up to 15 taste bonus)

| # | Dimension | Pts | Evidence required |
|---|-----------|-----|-------------------|
| 1 | Search & viewing demand | 20 | YT Data API: top-result view counts, recent-upload view velocity; web-researched volume/trend evidence with URLs |
| 2 | Competition attainability | 20 | YT API top-result audit: can small/new channels (<50k subs, <2yr) be found ranking in the niche's keywords? |
| 3 | Ad RPM potential | 15 | Sourced RPM range for the niche (research/niches/rpm-*.md) |
| 4 | Speed to YPP (1k subs + 4k watch-hrs) | 10 | Proof-channel trajectories: months-to-threshold for recent faceless channels in/near niche |
| 5 | Production simplicity at cadence | 15 | Format decomposition: can one person ship 1–2/wk faceless? (assets available, script-driven) |
| 6 | Topic depth / long-term sustainability | 10 | Enumerable content map: ≥100 plausible titles; evergreen vs news-cycle balance |
| 7 | Originality / policy defensibility | 10 | Passes reused-content/inauthentic-content rules by design (research/niches/ypp-policy.md) |
| T | Taste bonus | +15 max | Matches the taste-bonus rubric distilled from the 6 reference channels (research/taste-profiles/taste-signals.md) |

Scoring is comparative within the field; every score cites its evidence file or source-log ID.
A candidate with no verifiable demand evidence cannot score >5 on dimension 1, regardless of narrative appeal.

## Kill criteria (auto-fail regardless of total)
- Only mega-channels (>500k subs) rank across all its core keywords → fails G10 premise.
- Format cannot be produced solo at 1–2/wk → fails G12.
- Niche RPM evidence puts $2,000/mo out of reach at plausible view volumes within 6 months → fails G11.
- Format is inherently compilation/reused-content → fails G6.

## Process
1. Divergent generation: 12–14 ideas across history, gaming, AI, travel + intersections (G9).
2. Rapid evidence pass on all → score. 
3. Top 3–4 advance to deep validation (proof channels, keyword sampling).
4. Judge panel + skeptic pass before DECISION.md.

### Verdict: **SURVIVES-WITH-MODIFICATIONS**

Why it survives: the two facts that would kill it don't exist. (1) The clone charge
against the *taste references* fails on subject matter — Ancient Indy and Atlas Adam are
ancient-history channels; C14's subjects, sources, and claims are modern/geopolitical,
and §(c) shows the citation-anchored version is fully buildable (5/5 sampled topics
anchor to named statutes, UNSC resolutions, UNESCO listings, and prosecuted cases), so
the Atlas-Adam epistemic trap is avoidable by design. (2) The policy exposure is real
but entirely of the mitigable class, at a cadence (1–2/wk) that the lane's own
cadence-compatible proof (Paint Explainer, 4.1/mo, 1.95M subs) demonstrates is viable
without mass-production velocity. Demand and trajectory evidence remain the strongest in
the field [FILE: finalist-validation.md]. No auto-kill criterion is tripped.

Required modifications (without these, treat as KILL at launch gate):

1. **Ship the differentiation wedge as product, not prose, from video 1:** the Entry
   File on-screen dossier device (access status / named legal basis / penalty on record
   / last documented entry) + the "one legal way in" closing beat on every segment +
   the myth-audit beat. These must be visible in thumbnails/packaging grammar
   (dossier/stamp/permit visual identity), not just in the body — this is the
   Guardrail-6 compliance mechanism AND the author-input defense in one.
2. **Break the template before scale:** ≥3 structurally distinct episode types
   (countdown / single-place deep file / access-calendar special) in the first 10
   videos; cap cadence at 1–2/wk permanently (never chase Ancient Indy's 27/mo).
3. **Documented-only topic filter, enforced:** every roster entry requires a named
   legal instrument or official source fetched and archived before scripting; entries
   that only work on lore ("Google blurs it", hauntings) are excluded or explicitly
   framed as myth-audits. Primary-document fetch time must be line-itemed in the
   14–18 hr budget.
4. **Resolve the recombination exposure:** place-reuse cap (no place reappears within
   N videos), and a depth ledger that tracks the ~120-place pool honestly — with
   single-place deep files carrying the long tail, not recycled countdowns.
5. **Pre-launch policy verifications:** Google Earth Studio commercial terms; CC-only
   photo audit workflow; "Altered content" disclosure protocol for any synthetic
   voice/reconstruction; named narrator persona established from video 1.

## 3. TOP 4 Shortlist

### 1. C01 — The Ledger Breaks (95.33)
**Why it advanced:** The only candidate all three judges scored 95+. It uniquely
stacks the field's best revenue triangle (top of the $4–9 history band via
finance-adjacency, so ~$2K/mo at roughly 333K views/mo) on the audit's hottest lane:
2.9M-view top demand, and *repeated* recent from-zero breakouts (Madmam 0→145K in
~16 mo, Financial Historian 0→161K in <12 mo, Wealth Records 0→27.8K in ~6 mo), with
a 1,720-sub channel ranking #3 at 121K views. Cleanest policy profile (all-original
assets) and 95+ enumerable titles.
**Weakest dimension:** RPM (10.67/15, 71%) — the entire "finance-history tops the
band" thesis rests on LOW-confidence snippets; secondarily, the visible rank-4–6
clone-slop wave proves undifferentiated entrants die in this lane.
**Phase 3 must prove:** (a) re-verified history/finance RPM from fetched or
creator-primary sources at ≥$6; (b) keyword-level sampling that sub-10K channels rank
across ≥10 planned launch titles, not just the one probe; (c) a differentiation
wedge (forensic balance-sheet visual identity) that the failed clones visibly lack.

### 2. C11 — The Complete AI Stack (94.33)
**Why it advanced:** The audit's only triple-green — sub-10K channels holding 3.2M
and 1.3M-view videos in the exact space, SetupsAI 0→542K subs since 2024-04, the
per-profession "complete workflow" positioning genuinely unoccupied (everything
ranking is a listicle), and the field's best RPM band ($8–20 AI/B2B, Tier B): at
even $10, $2K/mo needs only ~200K views/mo.
**Weakest dimension:** YPP speed (6.33/10, 63%) — W5 applies in full: exhaustive
search-guide formats convert views to subs at ~0.68x (Recommended Playing retired),
so the 1,000-sub gate may lag the watch-hour gate badly. Depth (7.0) is next: 12–18
month tool-churn decay plus unverified per-profession keyword volume.
**Phase 3 must prove:** (a) RPM re-verification that the $8–20 band survives contact
with creator-reported data; (b) keyword volume across ≥15 professions (thin
professions collapse the 100+ title map); (c) a credible sub-conversion mechanism
(narrator persona, series hooks) that beats the 0.68x reference; (d) refresh-cycle
economics under tool churn.

### 3. C05 — Sunk Cost (88.33)
**Why it advanced:** The field's most extreme attainability datapoint — a
125-subscriber channel holding rank 4 with 153,875 views — plus Selwen's 545K on
58.6K subs, robust 1.5M top-end demand, and gaming's only credible above-band RPM
story (business-of-gaming $5–8, finance-adjacent advertisers). Sustainable 15–20
hr/video own-capture-plus-charts pipeline; topics renew every earnings season.
**Weakest dimension:** RPM (8.67/15, 58%) — the $5–8 claim is the least-corroborated
of the shortlist's RPM theses (single vidIQ snippet class), and if it lands at the
generic gaming $2–4, the required view volume roughly doubles.
**Phase 3 must prove:** (a) that business-of-gaming content actually monetizes at
$5+ (find creator-reported data); (b) that the dollar-figure framing wins against
adjacent incumbents (Wha Happun?, Death of a Game) on fresh keywords, i.e. the
125-sub case is a lane property, not a one-off; (c) topic-pool depth beyond the
famous ~20 collapses to the 100-title bar.

### 4. C06 — Patch Verdict (88.33)
**Why it advanced:** Best rankability in the entire audit (671-sub, 13-month channel
at rank #2; 5,850 subs with 480K views; 47K subs with 1.64M) on a template that
re-mints the whole catalog every January, with the cheapest, policy-cleanest
pipeline in the field (100% own capture, sustainable 2/wk) and elite depth (9.0/10).
Purchase-intent "worth buying" framing claims the top of the gaming band ($5–8,
Tier B).
**Weakest dimension:** YPP speed (5.00/10, 50%) — W5 head-on: its own audit proof
case (TimeWellPlayed) sits at 671 subs after 13 months *despite ranking #2*, so the
1,000-sub gate is the binding constraint; January's claimed RPM trough ($1.50–3)
coincides with the year-rollover demand peak.
**Phase 3 must prove:** (a) proof-channels in this query family that actually
cleared 1,000 subs + 4K hours inside ~6 months (if none exist, the concept fails the
brief's timeline even with good views); (b) purchase-intent RPM verification,
including the seasonality trough claim; (c) per-title demand distribution — the
probe's per-video views are moderate, so the model needs many mid-hits, not one
breakout.

**First alternate — C14 The No-Go Atlas (86.00):** field-best demand (11.5M) and YPP
proof (Hidden Planet Docs 0→66.9K subs, 862K-view video, in 5 months) but the
shortlist's worst RPM (6.67) and originality (6.00: Ancient Indy clone scrutiny under
Guardrail 6, mega-channel farming). Promote only if a shortlist member fails its
Phase 3 proofs.

## 4. Judge Disagreements

**No candidate shows a >15-point total spread between judges.** The panel is
unusually convergent because all three scored from the same Tier A audit. The only
apparent double-digit gap — C16 at 79 (REV) vs 68 (OPS) — was an arithmetic error in
the REVENUE sheet (dimensions sum to 69); corrected, C16's spread is 2 points.
Largest real spreads, all ≤5.5, and what they mean:

- **C08 Delisted, 5.5 (GRO 83.5 vs OPS 78):** growth prizes the 2.87M/1.63M demand
  and 10.2K-sub breakout; operator discounts the structural reused-content exposure
  (entries about unplayable games depend on archived third-party trailers). Both are
  right — this is a genuine risk/reward gap that Phase 3 would resolve only via a
  policy-safe asset plan, but C08 does not advance.
- **C02 Margin of Failure, 5.5 (REV 77 vs GRO 82.5):** growth credits young-channel
  entry (Industrial Evolution, 7 mo, top-8); revenue notes the best young proof is
  only 7K subs/28K views — evidence of a door ajar, not a breakout, at mid-band RPM.
- **C11, 5 (GRO 97 vs OPS 92):** the triple-green audit vs the operator taxes
  (per-profession credibility research, professional-audience blowback, tool-churn
  decay, W5). Interpretation: C11's upside is evidential, its risks are executional
  — exactly what deep validation is for.
- **C06, 4 (GRO 91 vs REV/OPS 87):** growth weights rankability hardest;
  revenue/operator both dock the same thing (W5 at the 1,000-sub gate), which is
  why YPP speed is the dimension C06 must clear in Phase 3.

Chair's read: the panel's variance concentrates in exactly two evidence gaps —
Tier B RPM confidence and W5 sub-conversion — which are therefore the two mandatory
proof burdens for every Phase 3 candidate.

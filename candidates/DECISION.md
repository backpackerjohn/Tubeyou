# DECISION — The Channel We Build

**Date:** 2026-07-19 · **Decided by:** full evidence chain (16-candidate field → 3-judge panel →
6 skeptic attacks → API trajectory validation). Every input cited below exists in this repo.

## Winner: C14 — "The No-Go Atlas" (working name; final brand in Phase 7)

**Concept (post-skeptic, modified):** A faceless long-form documentary channel about the world's
forbidden, restricted, and sealed places — **built as an inverted travel guide with a
citation-anchored dossier format**. Genres: travel × history (Guardrail 9 compliant).

### Why it won (evidence chain)

1. **Largest demand in the audited field.** Top probe results: 11.5M views (Top Fives), a 3.68M/2.10M/2.07M
   series (The Paint Explainer), 2.57M (Trust Me Bro). [tier-a-audit.md §C14]
2. **The field's best from-zero proof channel.** Hidden Planet Docs: created 2026-02-22, faceless,
   31–44 min docs, 66.9K subs at ~5 months, recent-15 mean 62.9K views/video (median-honest
   ex-outlier mean 34.9K), run-rate 293K–570K views/mo. YPP thresholds cleared inside its first
   months. [finalist-validation.md]
3. **Cadence-compatible scale proof.** The Paint Explainer: ~4.1 uploads/month (our 1–2/week band),
   faceless, 1.95M subs in 3 years, recent mean ~725K views/video. [finalist-validation.md]
4. **Quantified downside.** Untouched Mysteries: same niche, 15 months, ~2.1K views/video —
   packaging quality, not participation, is the variable. [finalist-validation.md]
5. **Credibility floor passes.** 5/5 sampled topics anchor to primary/official sources (UNESCO,
   UNSC resolutions, national laws, prosecuted cases). Atlas-Adam epistemics are a choice, not a
   necessity. [skeptic-reports.md, C14 policy section]
6. **Taste fit.** Engine A hooks, templatable title machine, discovery-driven traffic, 20–40 min
   watch-time format — high scores on rubric C1–C5 [taste-signals.md], with the differentiation
   duty handled by the wedge below.

### The honest revenue statement (Guardrail 11, as modified by skeptics)

The strict "6-month $2,000/mo" clause is **not evidence-supported for any candidate in the field**
— including this one. The defensible projection (detailed model in Phase 10):

- Month 6: **$450–1,500/mo** (at 6 uploads/mo, ex-outlier per-video economics, $3–5 RPM Tier B band)
- **$2,000/mo crossing: months 8–9 (good case with ≥1 outlier-class hit), months 10–14 (median case)**
- Floor case: Untouched-Mysteries outcome, ~$60–160/mo → caught early by the tripwire below

C14 was chosen because it *fails the 6-month clause by the smallest margin on the best evidence*;
runner-up C01's honest crossing was months 8–12+ with weaker per-video economics. Per the brief's
arc §11: the red team demanded the honest number; this is it, stated before launch, not after.

### Eliminated (with the number that killed each)

- **C01 The Ledger Breaks** (runner-up): best proof channels average 3.5–6.7K views per long-form
  video at 3–7x our cadence → month-6 $2K unreachable; months 8–12+ with weaker economics than C14.
- **C11 Complete AI Stack**: every big-view exhibit is Shorts (10–14s), inactive, or stale; zero
  active long-form proof. Worst view→sub conversion in field (156–325 views/sub).
- **C05 Sunk Cost**: documented gaming RPM $2–4 → needs 500K–1M views/mo; 3–6x best observed.
- **C06 Patch Verdict**: killed by skeptic #4 — revenue math fails at every documented RPM point;
  both YPP-speed datapoints negative (671 subs @ 13 months; 5.8K subs @ 7 years).
- **C04, C10, C12** killed earlier (big-channel lock-out; intent mismatch; depth) — see scoring-matrix.md.

### What Phase 5 must now prove (Guardrail 10)

Each of the 10 launch videos targets a specific keyword/topic validated by: demand-proxy metrics
(documented in keyword map; vidIQ unavailable — see build log), plus the YouTube Data API audit:
top-ranking videos, their channel sizes, ages, and view counts, with small/new channels present.
Any keyword where only mega-channels rank fails and is replaced.

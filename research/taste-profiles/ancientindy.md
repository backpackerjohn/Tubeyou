## Why the Angle Works (inference, grounded in fetched titles/stats)

1. **"Forbidden" reframing of history:** Every video converts archaeology into a thriller — the subject isn't the site, it's why you're not allowed to see it. This turns educational content into mystery/horror-adjacent content with a much broader browse audience.
2. **Infinitely templatable:** {10} + {ancient place type: mines/caves/tombs/tunnels/walls/labyrinths/catacombs/monasteries/islands/hospitals} + {prohibition/fear clause}. The template supports the ~daily cadence without creative exhaustion.
3. **Hour-long runtime = watch-time engine:** ~60-minute videos likely serve the "background listening / fall-asleep documentary" use case; even mediocre CTR compounds into large total watch time (inference).
4. **Volume as a lottery strategy:** With a 110x view spread, one breakout (135,910 views on the Mines video vs 18.4k average) pays for a week of average uploads and feeds subscribers back into the catalog.
5. **Credibility anchor:** Sensational titles but descriptions citing real publications (Nature) let it ride the mystery angle without fully tipping into debunkable clickbait (inference from one sampled description).

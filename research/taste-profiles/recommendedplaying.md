## Why the Angle Works (inference, grounded in fetched titles/stats)

1. **Search-intent alignment.** Titles are phrased exactly as players search ("All Merchant Requests Guide - Resident Evil 4 (2023)"). The 329 lifetime views-per-sub ratio and the 120x per-video view spread both indicate discovery is driven by search/suggested traffic for specific games, not by a subscriber audience.
2. **Riding new-release demand with evergreen depth.** The biggest recent hit (237,440 views — Clockwork Castellan Locations, published 2023-04-04) landed within weeks of the RE4 remake's March 2023 launch. The MH Rise: Sunbreak progression guides (72k–137k views each) similarly shipped during that expansion's active window. Timing a definitive guide to a hot game's launch window is the channel's clearest overperformance pattern.
3. **Completeness as a moat.** A 7h22m all-achievements guide with 55k views, and a 6h27m platinum guide with 23k views, signal to viewers (and plausibly the algorithm) that no follow-up video is needed. "All X" framing beats fragmentary guides for the same query.
4. **Series templates.** "Equipment Progression Guide" repeated across 5 weapons is a repeatable template: one research pipeline, many videos, and each ranks for its own weapon-specific query — a production-efficiency pattern directly relevant to a 1–2/week faceless channel.
5. **Underserved-game bets have a floor.** Deep-cut games (SMT: Digital Devil Saga, FF5, Dead Rising 3) pull 2k–20k views — modest, but nonzero on old titles years after release, showing the evergreen tail of guide content.

**Caveat:** the channel is retired at 58.5k subs after ~9 years, and its sub conversion is weak. As a taste reference it demonstrates search-first guide demand, not a subscriber-growth engine.

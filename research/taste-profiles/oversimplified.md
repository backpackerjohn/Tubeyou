## Why the Angle Works (inference, grounded in fetched titles/stats)

1. **The brand is the value proposition.** "OverSimplified" pre-frames every video: maximum-effort topics, minimum-effort viewing. Viewers click the promise, not the topic.
2. **Evergreen search-and-recommend topics.** Every title is a perennial curriculum topic (French Revolution, Civil War, Cold War, Henry VIII) — school-adjacent demand that never expires. The 2018–2019 uploads (Cold War Pt1: 74.0M; American Revolution Pt1: 63.1M) kept compounding views for 6+ years.
3. **Comedy differentiates commodity history.** Straight history explainers are abundant; comedic animation makes retellings of well-covered events original (relevant to Guardrail 6 thinking).
4. **Quality over cadence works at scale but is not a launch model.** ~0.2–0.3 videos/month with 19M+ views per release is the opposite of a new-channel cadence strategy; the taste signal to take is the angle (irreverent, dense, story-first history), not the release schedule.
5. **Same-day multi-part drops** turn one topic into 2–3 videos and immediate binge sessions (French Rev Pt1+Pt2, Punic War Pt1+Pt2 each pulled 19M–50M per part).

## Summary (provisional)

**Long-form history content, mostly-US/EN (Tier-1) audience: roughly $4–$9 RPM
(creator take), with the snippet cluster centering on $4–$6 and optimistic
sources quoting $5–$10/$5–$12.** Non-Tier-1 (South/Southeast Asia-heavy)
audiences reportedly drop to ~$1–$2.50 RPM. History sits mid-tier: above general
entertainment/gaming, below finance ($9–$11+ RPM) and tech/B2B.

## Nuances (snippet-level, unverified)

- **Geography dominates.** Same content: Tier-1 (US/UK/CA/AU/Western EU) ~$4–$9 RPM vs ~$1–$2.50 RPM for South/Southeast-Asia-heavy audiences. A "70% US viewers" channel earns a multiple of a global-south-heavy one.
- **Sub-niche tilt raises RPM:** history blended with economics/finance/current events (e.g. "economic history of X", war-economy angles) reportedly lands at the top of the range because it pulls finance-adjacent advertisers. Pure ancient-history/mythology storytelling tends toward the lower half.
- **Advertiser mix** for history: streaming/documentary services, book publishers, online learning (Skillshare/Brilliant-type), historical strategy games, museums/cultural institutions — mid-tier payers, above entertainment, below finance/B2B/software.
- **Demographics help:** history viewers skew educated, 25–54, disposable income — a premium demo — which is why history outperforms gaming/entertainment despite no direct purchase intent.
- **Huge intra-niche spread:** AIR Media-Tech's education data shows an 8.4x top-quartile vs bottom-quartile CPM gap ($19.50 vs $2.31) — channel-level RPM depends heavily on watch time, mid-rolls (8+ min videos), seasonality (Q4 high, Q1 trough), and ad-friendliness (graphic war content can get limited ads).
- **RPM vs CPM:** most niche lists quote RPM (creator take, post-YouTube-split, averaged over all views incl. unmonetized). Where a source quotes CPM (AIR's $5.91 education Studio CPM), effective RPM is lower — roughly CPM x monetized-view share x ~55% creator share.

## Bottom line

Provisional planning range for a long-form, US/EN-heavy history channel: **$4–$9 RPM**, plan conservatively at $4–$6, upside to $8–$12 with mid-rolls, 20+ min videos, and finance/economics-adjacent framing. Confidence LOW — no source could be fetch-verified from this environment; re-verify from an unrestricted network before using these numbers in the revenue model.

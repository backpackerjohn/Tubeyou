## Summary estimate (long-form, mostly-US/EN audience)

- **Working range: ~$3–$8 RPM** (creator take, per 1,000 long-form views) for general
  travel guides / destination explainers with a majority US/tier-1 audience.
- Plausible upside to **~$9–$14 RPM** for finance-adjacent sub-niches (travel credit
  cards/points, cost-of-living and moving-abroad content, travel insurance angles) and
  peak booking season; one aggregator (isthischannelmonetized) reportedly claims
  **$9–$20** for travel audiences in the US/Mexico, which is the high outlier among
  sources seen.
- Downside to **~$1–$4 RPM** off-season (Jan–Feb), for vlog/entertainment-leaning travel
  content, or with significant developing-market viewership.

These bounds are consistent across several independent aggregator summaries but are NOT
backed by any fetched page or creator screenshot.

## Nuances

- **RPM vs CPM**: Sources mix the two freely. CPM is advertiser cost; creator RPM is
  roughly 55% of ad revenue spread over all views (including unmonetized ones), so a
  $11–$14 travel CPM plausibly maps to ~$4–$8 RPM — consistent with the working range.
- **Audience geography is the biggest lever**: multiple summaries repeat a 5–8x gap
  between US/tier-1 and South-Asian/developing-market audiences for identical content.
  The summary range above assumes mostly-US/EN viewership.
- **Seasonality is strong in travel**: booking season (Apr–Aug, plus pre-holiday) can
  roughly double CPMs vs the January–February trough. Annual averages hide a ~2-3x
  intra-year swing.
- **Sub-niche spread**:
  - Higher: travel credit cards/points, travel insurance, cost-of-living / moving-abroad
    / expat finance (finance-adjacent advertisers), luxury travel.
  - Middle: destination guides, geography explainers (educational framing may also help).
  - Lower: pure travel vlogs / entertainment-style travel, mass-market budget travel.
- **AdSense is often the minor revenue stream** for travel creators: tourism-board deals
  (reported $5k–$30k/campaign), hotel/airline partnerships, and booking affiliates
  (Booking.com, Travelpayouts, GetYourGuide) reportedly dominate top-creator income.
- **No creator-primary data obtained**: zero income reports, screenshots, or Reddit
  threads with concrete travel RPMs could be retrieved. Until at least 2-3 such data
  points are verified, the $3–$8 range should be treated as a prior, not a finding.

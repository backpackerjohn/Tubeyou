## RPM vs CPM handling

- CPM = what advertisers pay per 1,000 monetized ad impressions; RPM = creator revenue per
  1,000 total views (after YouTube's ~45% cut, includes non-ad revenue, diluted by
  unmonetized views). A US-tech CPM of $20–$35 typically lands as $8–$18 RPM in creator
  dashboards. Where the table says CPM, the creator-take estimate applies that conversion
  and is labeled as such.

## Nuances (from snippets — unverified)

- **Sub-niche gradient:** "AI for business / make money with AI / AI + finance" angles command
  the top of the range (finance-adjacent advertisers). Pure AI news/entertainment recaps sit
  lower (more general-audience advertisers). AI coding/dev-tool tutorials benefit from
  developer-tool advertisers (JetBrains, Copilot-type products).
- **Geography:** all figures assume majority US/UK/CA/AU viewership; heavy non-Tier-1
  traffic can cut RPM by 3–10x.
- **Saturation:** multiple snippets note the "make money with AI" angle is heavily saturated
  in 2026 and YouTube throttles low-quality/undisclosed AI content; AI-content disclosure
  failures risk YPP suspension.
- **Sponsorships dwarf AdSense** in this niche per snippets ($5–15K/mo sponsorship vs
  $2–5K/mo AdSense at ~100K subs) — RPM understates total monetization for AI-tool channels
  with affiliate/sponsor potential.

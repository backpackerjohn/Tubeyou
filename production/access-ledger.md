## What this ledger is

The single source of truth for every place/zone the channel may cover. No place enters a script
outline until its row here has (a) a **named legal instrument** and (b) a source-quality tier that
is honest about what has actually been checked. This ledger is also the enforcement mechanism for
the **place-reuse cap**: every countdown appearance and deep-file use is logged in the Usage columns
(added at first use), and no place may reappear in any video within **10 uploads** of its last
appearance, with a maximum of **2 countdown appearances per rolling 12 months** per place.

## Verification tiers (be honest — M4)

- **Tier B-verified (date)** — claims spot-checked via WebSearch at search-result level on the noted
  date (this session: 2026-07-19; or via skeptic-reports.md §c, same date). Primary page NOT fetched.
  Still requires a primary-document fetch + archive before scripting.
- **Unverified** — general-knowledge entry. The named instrument is the *believed* basis and is
  flagged where confidence is lower; nothing in an Unverified row may appear on an Entry File card
  until independently verified and archived. If verification fails, the row is cut or reframed as a
  myth-audit.
- **Tier A (target state)** — primary document fetched, archived to `/production/citations/<place>/`,
  quote + page reference logged. Required before the place appears on screen. (No rows are Tier A
  yet; this is the pre-script gate.)

Do not invent instruments. Where a row says "instrument to confirm," that is a research task, not a
citation.

1. **Pre-script gate:** a place must reach Tier A (primary doc fetched + archived) before its Entry
   File card is built. Tier B rows may be *slated*, never *scripted*.
2. **Usage tracking:** on first use, append columns `Last used (video #)` and `Countdown count
   (rolling 12 mo)`. Enforce: no reappearance within 10 uploads; max 2 countdown slots/place/year.
3. **Status-change watch:** rows 4, 8, 10, 25, 26, 29, 40, 43 have live dated arcs — check for news
   monthly; each dated change feeds the Access-Calendar special format (M3c).
4. **Failed-keyword discipline:** rows marked "keyword FAILED" (2, 3, 5, 25, 33, 38) are segment
   material only, per launch-keyword-validation.md kills — never standalone videos without a fresh
   Guardrail-10 pass.
5. **Sensitivity screen:** rows 19 and 39 (and any death-led framing) get an ad-suitability and tone
   review before slating (skeptic-reports.md C14-revenue mod 2).
6. **Count honesty:** the documented pool is ~100–130 distinct places (skeptic-reports.md §d); this
   ledger seeds 46. Expansion target: +5 rows/month from research days, each entering as Unverified
   until checked.

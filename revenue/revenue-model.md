# Revenue Model — Launch to Month 9 (and the honest path to $2,000/month)

Built 2026-07-19. Every input is either (a) traced to API-verified data in this repo, or (b) an
explicitly labeled **modeled assumption** resting on Tier B (snippet-verified) RPM evidence. The
brief's Guardrail 11 asks for a credible path to $2,000/mo within 6 months; the adversarial process
(skeptic-reports.md, C14 revenue section) found that claim not evidence-supported for ANY candidate,
and the arc §11 instruction — "if the honest answer is a longer timeline, say so" — governs here.

**Headline: month-6 revenue $430–1,500 (scenario-dependent); $2,000/mo crossing in month 8–9 (good
case), month 10–14 (median). The floor case ($60–160/mo) is caught by the month-2 tripwire.**

## Floor case (Untouched Mysteries outcome)

~2,100 views/video → ~13K views/mo at month 6 → **$35–60/mo**; sub gate not cleared until month
9–12. **The M6 tripwire exists for exactly this branch:** if no video exceeds 10K views by upload
8–10 (month 2), packaging is overhauled or the project is killed before sunk cost compounds.

## Levers (in order of leverage)

1. **Packaging quality** — the niche's 28x outcome spread (HPD vs Untouched Mysteries) dwarfs every
   other variable. Hence the A/B title/thumbnail protocol and tripwire.
2. **RPM realization** — mid-rolls on 20–30 min videos, US/EN targeting, December catalog push.
   Pre-launch action item M8: re-verify RPM with ≥2 creator-reported datapoints (impossible from
   this environment's network; documented as open).
3. **Outlier frequency** — countdown formats (K2, K9 class) are the documented outlier generators in
   this niche (11.5M / 9.7M / 2.5M heads); keep ≥1 in every 6-video block.
4. **Catalog compounding** — evergreen topics (all 10 launch keywords are evergreen) keep earning;
   the tail assumption (35% carryover, decaying) is the model's quiet workhorse — labeled inference.

## What would falsify this model

- RPM realizing below $3 sustained (→ crossing slips ~proportionally; at $2, month 11 base-case
  revenue is $980, crossing ~month 14+).
- Views/sub conversion materially worse than 1/120 (→ YPP delayed; every month of delay pushes all
  revenue right by a month).
- Tripwire branch: no >10K video by upload 10.

Secondary upside NOT in the model (per Guardrail 11 they are never the plan): sponsorships
(geography/VPN/atlas-adjacent advertisers), affiliate (books/maps), licensing. These begin to be
realistic only after ~50K subs (inference).
